The decision before you press play

Find your next watch and keep your place.

You have 45 minutes, a few subscriptions, and want something light. Pick a title, see its watch options, and save your place for next time. Watch It helps you discover and track movies and TV. No video files needed; playback happens on your streaming service.

Sample content only. Titles, artwork, offers and progress below are fictional. No API keys, login or live catalog are used. Changes last only until you reset or close this page and never reach a personal library.

Make it your own

The self-hosted app needs Docker and your own TMDB API key. In Settings > API keys > TMDB API key, follow the link to get a key, paste it in the field, and choose Verify and save TMDB key. Then choose your region and subscriptions. OMDb adds optional ratings. No streaming-service passwords are needed.

One shared library per installation. Separate hosted accounts and private libraries are a proposal, not an available feature.
